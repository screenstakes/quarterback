    Questions

    Before you rely on this

    Is Quarterback official IRS software?

    No. Quarterback is independent software, not affiliated with or endorsed by the IRS. It's a planning tool that estimates federal tax based on what you enter.

    Is the estimate the exact amount I must pay?

    No. It's a simplified planning estimate. Your actual tax liability depends on details this tool does not model, including many deductions and credits. Confirm actual payments using IRS Form 1040-ES, IRS guidance, tax software, or a qualified tax professional.

    Where is my financial data stored?

    In this prototype, your ledger data is stored only in your current browser's local storage. There is no account, server, or cloud backup.

    What happens if I clear my browser data or change devices?

    Your records can be permanently lost. Export a CSV or JSON backup regularly using the export tools on this page.

    Does Quarterback account for business expenses?

    Yes. You can record expenses alongside income, and the estimate is based on net business profit (income minus expenses), not gross income.

    Does it support deductions and tax credits?

    Only in a limited way. It applies the federal standard deduction and lets you enter an expected credit amount as a simple subtraction. It does not calculate itemized deductions, the Qualified Business Income (Section 199A) deduction, or most other credits.

    Does it include state taxes?

    No. This estimate is federal only.

    Does recording a payment send money to the IRS?

    No. Recording an estimated payment here only updates Quarterback's internal calculation. Make actual payments through irs.gov/payments or another official channel.

    Why can my actual tax bill differ from this estimate?

    This tool omits capital gains rates, itemized deductions, most credits, the Alternative Minimum Tax, Net Investment Income Tax, retirement and health-insurance deductions, and several other rules described in the assumptions panel. Any of these can change your real liability.

    What is the estimated-tax safe harbor?

    Generally, penalties for underpaying estimated tax can often be avoided by paying at least 90% of the current year's tax, or 100% of the prior year's tax (110% if your prior-year income was high). Quarterback shows an informational comparison if you enter prior-year figures — this is not a guarantee and not legal advice.

    Is Pro available?

    Not yet. Pro features shown on this page are planned but not built. You can join the launch list on the pricing section.

    Can I export my records?

    Yes. You can export a CSV of your ledger and estimated payments, and a full JSON backup of your data, from the export section below.

    Who should not rely on this simplified estimate?

    Anyone with capital gains, rental income, complex deductions, multiple businesses, employees, retirement-plan contributions, health-insurance deductions for the self-employed, or other complex situations should use IRS worksheets, tax software, or a qualified tax professional instead of relying on this tool alone.
