    Frequently asked questions

    Is Quarterback official IRS software?
    No. Quarterback is independent software. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Internal Revenue Service, the Department of the Treasury or any other government agency. It is a planning tool that estimates federal tax from what you enter.
    Is the estimate the exact amount I have to pay?
    No. It is a simplified planning estimate. Your actual liability depends on rules this tool does not model, including most deductions and credits. Confirm actual payments using IRS Form 1040-ES, IRS guidance, tax software, or a qualified tax professional.
    Where is my financial data stored?
    In your current browser's local storage, on this device. There is no account, no server and no cloud backup. Quarterback makes no network requests at all after the page loads — it does not even load fonts from a third party.
    What happens if I clear my browser data or change devices?
    Your records can be permanently lost, and there is no way to recover them. Export a CSV or JSON backup regularly from the Settings tab. Private or incognito windows typically discard local storage when the window closes.
    Does the estimate account for business expenses?
    Yes. The estimate is based on net business profit — income minus the expenses you record — not on gross income. Recording an expense is not the same as it being deductible; that depends on your circumstances and the ordinary-and-necessary test.
    Does it support deductions and tax credits?
    Only in a limited way. It applies the federal standard deduction and lets you enter an expected credit amount as a flat subtraction. It does not calculate itemized deductions, the qualified business income deduction under section 199A, retirement contribution deductions, the self-employed health insurance deduction, or any specific credit.
    Does it include state or local tax?
    No. The estimate is federal only. If you live in a state with income tax, your total obligation will be higher than the figure shown here.
    Does recording a payment send money to the IRS?
    No. Recording a payment only updates Quarterback's internal arithmetic. Make actual payments through irs.gov/payments or another official channel.
    Why might my actual tax bill differ from this estimate?
    This tool omits capital gains rates, itemized deductions, most credits, the Alternative Minimum Tax, the Net Investment Income Tax, retirement and health-insurance deductions, the qualified business income deduction, and several other rules listed in full under the Estimate tab. Any one of them can move your real liability substantially.
    What is the estimated-tax safe harbor?
    Broadly, underpayment penalties can often be avoided by paying at least 90% of the current year's tax or 100% of the prior year's tax — 110% if your prior-year adjusted gross income was above the high-income threshold. Quarterback shows an informational comparison if you enter prior-year figures. That comparison is not a guarantee and not legal advice.
    Who should not rely on this estimate?
    Anyone with capital gains, rental income, multiple businesses, employees, an S corporation or partnership interest, retirement plan contributions, self-employed health insurance, foreign income, farming or fishing income, or complex deductions. Use IRS worksheets, tax software, or a qualified professional instead.
    Is Pro available?
    No. The Pro features described on the Pricing tab are planned, not built. Nothing is charged and no payment method is collected anywhere in this application.
    The full legal position is set out in the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. Both are worth reading before you use the estimate for anything that matters.

In one well-known case, a German court held that embedding Google Fonts in that way was an unlawful transfer of a visitor's IP address under the GDPR (Landgericht München I, case 3 O 17493/20, 20 January 2022). Rather than disclose such a transfer, we avoid it.

Whoever hosts the Service — and any content delivery network or your own internet service provider in between — can see the network-level metadata inherent in that request, typically your IP address, the time, the requested file and your user agent. That is a property of the web, not of Quarterback, it happens before any of our code runs, and it is outside our control. It is also unconnected to anything you type: the host sees that a page was fetched, never what was entered into it afterwards.

    • Delete a single record — use the delete action on any ledger or payment row.

    11Security

    The strongest security property of the Service is architectural: data that is never transmitted cannot be intercepted in transit, and data we never receive cannot be exposed by a breach of ours. There is no user database to breach.

    That is not the same as saying your records are secure, and it would be dishonest to imply otherwise. You should understand the following:

    • Local storage is not encrypted. Records are stored as plain text within your browser profile.
    • There is no login. Anyone who can use your unlocked device and browser profile can read, edit, export and delete everything.
    • Other software on your device may be able to read it, including malware and some browser extensions with broad permissions.
    • Shared, public and managed devices are a poor choice for this data. So is a synced browser profile if you do not want the records replicated to your other devices by the browser itself.
    • Exported CSV and JSON files are unencrypted and carry no protection once they leave the Service.

    Practical steps that actually help: use full-disk encryption, lock your screen, keep your browser and operating system updated, be conservative about extension permissions, and store exported backups somewhere you would be comfortable storing a bank statement.

    No method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information held on a device we do not control.
